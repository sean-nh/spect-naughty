SPECTR-ACULAR

Plays along with whatever your radio is playing. Tap Start listening and allow audio access when asked.

Privacy

Spectr-Acular does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No internet access. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off this device. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Audio access. Android requires this permission to let an app analyse the sound being played. The app does that in memory to draw the graphics. Nothing is recorded or saved. If the device's audio cannot be read directly, the microphone is used instead, and Android shows its microphone indicator while it is.
  • Song info (optional notification access). The app only looks at the title, artist, cover and play state that music apps publish, and sends play, pause, next and previous. It does not read, store or send your notifications or messages.
  • What stays on the device. Your display settings and a short log of app events for the Diagnostics screen. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Your control. You can change or withdraw any permission in Android Settings, Apps, Spectr-Acular.

The full text is in PRIVACY.md in the project's GitHub repository.
